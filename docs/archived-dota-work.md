# Archived Dota work: excluded from League evidence

**Scope correction, 30 September 2026:** these studies used Dota 2. The user did
not want the League research replaced with another game's experiments. The work
is inactive and must not be presented as progress, validation, training scale,
baselines or novelty evidence for League of Legends.

The original artifacts remain unchanged to preserve their source bindings and
negative results. Their presence in the repository does not make them active
League work. No new acquisition or experiment in this direction is planned.

| Historical study | Preserved result |
|---|---|
| [Seven-match replay audit](../reports/objective-onset-corpus-2026-09-30.md) | Reactive objective-completion and first-damage measurements on Dota replays |
| [Pre-contact feature studies](../reports/precontact-research-2026-09-30.md) | Two experimental waves, 500 candidates audited; both primary gates failed |
| [Command-stream study](../reports/command-anticipation-research-2026-09-30.md) | Commands did not beat the history control on the fresh Dota cohort |
| [History-plus-command follow-up](command-history-development-screen.md) | Development candidate tied the history control |
| [Objective-training studies](../reports/objective-training-research-2026-09-30.md) | 48 final neural fits; cooldown and shared-threshold candidates failed their gates |

The additional 750 audited matches and 7.56 million hero-state rows in the last
study were Dota data. They did not expand the audited Riot League cohort.
Draft PRs #67–#70 contain this historical branch of work and are not League
empirical evidence. The active scope and real League results are in
[league-research-scope.md](league-research-scope.md).
